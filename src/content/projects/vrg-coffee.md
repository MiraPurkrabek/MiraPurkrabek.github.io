I enjoy speciality coffee, and once built and hosted a simple public web app for rating the
coffees we brewed in our group at VRG. The frontend provided the web UI, deployed on Vercel,
and a Supabase database stored the coffees and their ratings. Like [Revie](/work/#revie),
it worked in a browser across operating systems and devices without any installation.

It was a small system I took from idea to deployment, but adoption stayed limited: people
in the group didn't really use it. I eventually retired the hosted version because keeping
Vercel and Supabase running for a hobby with little interest wasn't worth the cost.
