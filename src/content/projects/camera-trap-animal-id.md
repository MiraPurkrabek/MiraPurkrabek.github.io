A project led by Lukáš Picek (University of West Bohemia) identifying wild animals, particularly
lynxes, from camera-trap images. The contribution here is 2D pose estimation of the animals, which
improves identification accuracy — each lynx has a different fur texture on each side, and pose
estimates help align the correct side for comparison. Part of a larger effort applying computer
vision and AI to environmental protection.
